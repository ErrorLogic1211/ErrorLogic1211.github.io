I entered **RetinexDualV2** in the NTIRE 2026 challenges, held with the CVPR 2026 workshops:

- **4th place** in the Second Challenge on Day and Night Raindrop Removal for Dual-Focused Images. The challenge used the real-world Raindrop Clarity dataset; 168 teams registered and 17 submitted valid final solutions {% cite li2026ntire_raindrop %}.
- **5th place** in Track 2, Joint Denoising and Low-Light Image Enhancement (JDLLIE), of the Low Light Image Enhancement Challenge {% cite ciubotariu2026ntire_lowlight %}.

The method itself is described in the RetinexDualV2 paper {% cite kishawy2026retinexdualv2 %}.

Our team, **ITB Dehaze**, ranked **4th** in the NTIRE 2024 Dense and Non-Homogeneous Dehazing Challenge, held with the CVPR 2024 workshops. The challenge introduced DNH-HAZE, a dataset of 50 pairs of real outdoor images with dense, non-homogeneous haze and their haze-free counterparts. The results are summarized in the challenge report {% cite ancuti2024ntire %}.

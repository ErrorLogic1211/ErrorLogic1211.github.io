Our team, **Imagine**, finished **2nd overall** in the NTIRE 2025 Low Light Image Enhancement Challenge and achieved the **highest PSNR** on the test set (26.35 dB). The challenge drew 762 registered participants; the methods and final rankings are described in the challenge report {% cite liu2025ntire %}.

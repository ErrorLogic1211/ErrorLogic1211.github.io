Low-Light Novel View Synthesis Evaluation

Scenes were captured in the dark and reconstructed by different 3D methods. You will rate how good each result looks.

Before you start: please do this study on a laptop or desktop computer connected to Wi-Fi. The videos are large and need a big screen to judge fairly.

Duration~10 min
Questions17
Sections2
Scale1–5

Instructions

  1. Each question shows five versions of the same scene, labelled A to E. The methods behind the letters are hidden and shuffled on every question.
  2. Rate every version for overall visual quality: is the scene clearly visible, do colours and brightness look natural, and is it free of haze, blur, noise, floating blobs or flicker?
  3. Section 1 has looping videos that play in sync. Section 2 has still images.
  4. Click any video or image to view it larger; in the large view, the arrow keys switch between versions. You can give several versions the same score.
  5. Move between questions in any order: use Previous and Next, or click a question in the list on the left. Submit the assessment once every version is rated.
  6. Each rating is saved as soon as you click it, so you can close the page and continue later. Please keep your screen at full brightness.

Rating scale

5ExcellentLooks like a clean, well-lit photo or video.
4GoodMinor flaws you have to look for.
3FairNoticeable flaws, but the scene is clear.
2PoorObvious flaws that get in the way.
1BadHard to make out the scene.

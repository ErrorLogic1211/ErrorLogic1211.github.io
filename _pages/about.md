### Biography

I am a final-year Ph.D. candidate in Electrical and Computer Engineering at [McMaster University](https://www.mcmaster.ca/), Canada, supervised by [Prof. Jun Chen](https://www.ece.mcmaster.ca/~junchen/). My thesis, _Modelling the Physics of Degradation: Retinex-Based Restoration from 2D Images to 3D Scenes_, uses Retinex theory to model how images are degraded and to restore both 2D images and 3D scenes.

Before McMaster, I earned my B.Sc. in Computer Engineering from [Nile University](https://www.nu.edu.eg/), Egypt, where my graduation project on [federated learning for autonomous-vehicle lane segmentation](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-024-71187-8) became a _Scientific Reports_ paper. I also worked as a machine learning researcher at Helwan University with [Dr. Mahmoud M. Elmesalawy](https://scholar.google.com/citations?hl=en&user=mh_xQLUAAAAJ), and as a research assistant at the [Wireless Intelligent Network Center, Nile University](https://winc.nu.edu.eg/) with [Dr. Mohamed S. Darweesh](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=vzC9o50AAAAJ&hl=en). At McMaster, I have been a teaching assistant for courses in programming, algorithm design, computer architecture, and probability.

### Research Interests

- **3D vision:** 3D Gaussian Splatting and NeRFs for 3D reconstruction, restoration, and editing
- **Image restoration:** physically grounded models for low-light enhancement, deraining, dehazing, and deblurring at UHD resolution
